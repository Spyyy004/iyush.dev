1/ I tried to find Bruno Fernandes' replacement in Serie A using 10 years of data from Europe's top five leagues.

The most interesting result wasn't the shortlist. 🧵

2/ First, context. The same player produces ~27% more xG per minute at home, and ~14% less per step up in opponent strength.

But 0 of 4,665 players had a reliable personal home edge, and 0 of 23,022 estimates showed real "big-game" resistance. Context is shared, not a trait.

3/ So every profile was adjusted for opponents, venue and league, then compared within role.

Closest Serie A profiles to Bruno: Samardžić, Dybala, Chukwueze.

4/ But none is Bruno. His chance creation is 3.6 SD above his role average. The highest in Serie A: 1.9.

There's no statistical twin — only players who share parts of his game.

5/ Then the harder question: does looking like players who succeeded after a move predict success?

Tested on real league moves: no. Similarity added nothing detectable to the forecast.

6/ What worked: the player's own three-season history, age, both clubs, both leagues.

On 142 moves it never saw: typical error 0.096 vs 0.129 xG+xA/90 for "he'll do what he did".

7/ Applied to Man Utd on 1 June 2024: Zirkzee predicted 0.46, actual 0.47. Ugarte predicted 0.02, actual 0.14 — a miss on a known weakness, and on a player valued for defending the data can't see.

8/ Similarity finds candidates. History forecasts them. Neither sees everything.

Run Bruno (or anyone): https://iyush.dev/football/tools/similarity?player=bruno-fernandes&league=serie-a&seasons=3
