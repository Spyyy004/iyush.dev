# Analyst outreach

*Small, targeted list (15–30 people), not a blast. One finding, one link, no ask beyond "thought it might interest you". Personalise the first line from something they've published.*

## Who

| Group | Why them | Best link |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment analysts at clubs | transfer model + case study | case study |
| Performance analysts | home / opposition context | opposition or home-advantage study |
| Football data scientists (clubs, providers) | methodology, leakage control, locked tests | methodology |
| Heads of analytics | the system as a whole | /football |
| Data companies (event data, scouting platforms) | tools + what data is missing | similarity tool |
| Scouting / recruitment consultants | similarity vs prediction | transferability study |

Track: name · role · org · date sent · link sent · reply (y/n) · follow-up — in a simple sheet. One polite follow-up after 10 days, then stop.

## Message (default)

> Hey [name] — I recently finished a five-study project on how much football performance belongs to the player versus context, and built a couple of interactive recruitment tools around it.
>
> The most interesting part: player similarity looked useful descriptively but added essentially nothing to transfer prediction — the player's own history did the work.
>
> Thought it might be up your alley: https://iyush.dev/football/research/transferability

## Variants

- **Recruitment:** "…I also re-ran the model as of 1 June 2024 against Manchester United's window — its prediction for Zirkzee landed within 0.01 of his actual output, and it missed Ugarte on a weakness it had already documented: https://iyush.dev/football/case-studies/manchester-united-2024"
- **Performance / opposition:** "…the same player loses ~14% xG per step up in opponent strength, but I couldn't find a single reliable 'big-game player' in 23,022 estimates: https://iyush.dev/football/research/opposition"
- **Data science:** "…opponent strength comes from a sequential rating on pre-match odds (0 of 155,282 leakage checks changed a rating), and the transfer model was locked before its test seasons were opened: https://iyush.dev/football/methodology"
