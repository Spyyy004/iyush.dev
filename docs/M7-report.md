## 0. Where the brief and the locked output differ

Every headline number in the brief matches the locked Study 5 output:
- Zirkzee 0.46 → 0.47, rank 48 / 106;
- Ugarte 0.02 → 0.14;
- strikers 4 / 4, midfielders 5 / 6;
- locked test: 142 moves, MAE 0.096, R² 0.76, AUC 0.80.

Three framings in the brief would misstate the research, so the page follows the research instead:

1. **Ugarte (§9) is two limits, not one.** The brief blames only what attacking stats can't see. The research also names a **known bias**: the Premier League adjustment is a fixed amount, so central and defensive midfielders moving into the EPL are under-predicted by about 0.06. The page shows both, and says the research documented both before the outcome.
2. **The Study 3 chain (§17) isn't how the model works.** "Raw → context → adjusted output → transfer prediction" is not what happens. Study 5 found that **opposition and home context do not improve forecasts**; Model A uses three-season raw output, age, both clubs' strength and both leagues. The page says Study 3 explains why raw output needs context, but that for forecasting a move, the adjustment added nothing.
3. **Study 4 (§16) finds candidates; it doesn't forecast them.** The page keeps "Who looks like him? → What happens when he moves?" and adds that similarity **adds nothing to the forecast**. In this case study it builds the midfield shortlist; the forecast is Study 5's.

## 4. Leakage-control implementation

- **The page shows the research's protocol; it adds none of its own.** Model A was refitted on 334 moves whose first destination season ended by June 2024. Ranges come from out-of-sample errors in rolling folds 2019/20–2023/24. Inputs are history up to 2023/24 and United's 2023/24 club strength.
- **No forecast was recomputed.**
- **One subtle point, now disclosed in the drawer.** For "candidates who joined Premier League clubs", the research re-scores each player for the club he *actually* joined, so the choice of destination uses hindsight. The inputs are still June-2024 information (2023/24 club strength). The page says this.
- **Judging rule:** a forecast counts only with at least 450 minutes. Chiesa (95′) is shown as "Not judged".

## 5. Candidate-level rendering

- **Results:** all 11 Premier League joiners are shown: 5 strikers, 6 midfielders. Outside-range rows are flagged; signed players are highlighted.
- **Midfield bias:** the midfield text says the forecasts sit visibly low.
