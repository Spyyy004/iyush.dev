      About the ratings

      Each completed match gets 1 to 5 stars from what happened in it: goals and shots on target, how long the score stayed close, and late goals, comebacks, equalisers, red cards and penalties. It measures entertainment, not football quality, and it is the same formula for every competition. xG is not part of the rating.

      A partial rating means an input was missing for that match and the rating uses what was available. Matches that were postponed, or where the sources disagree on the result, are listed without a rating.

      Rating method mer-1.0.0 · calibration cal-2025-v1 (2025/26, frozen).

      Data: Understat and football-data.co.uk (domestic leagues), ESPN (Champions, Europa and Conference Leagues; qualifying rounds not included). Club crests are trademarks of their clubs; images via ESPN.
