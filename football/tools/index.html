Tools · Studies 04–05

Run the models yourself.

Two of the five studies ended in something you can use: a way to find players who play alike, and a way to estimate what survives a move. They answer different questions — and only one of them is a forecast.

Study 04 · Discovery

Player similarity

Who plays like him?

What it does
Finds the players in any top-five league whose context-adjusted profile — chance creation, involvement, shooting volume and shot profile — looks most like the player you pick, compared only within the same role.
What it can't do
Predict how a player will do after a move. Study 5 tested exactly that: similarity added nothing to the forecast. It also can't see defending, pace or anything outside attacking output.
Data
Profiles as of the 2025/26 season · every result precomputed with the Study 4 engine

Transfer calculator

Will his game travel?

What it does
Predicts a forward or midfielder's first-season + at a chosen club in another league, with an and the chance he keeps at least 75% of his output — and shows which inputs drive the number.
What it can't do
Price, fit, injuries or defensive work. Individual forecasts are uncertain (a typical 80% range is ±0.2), and low-output central midfielders moving into the Premier League are under-predicted.
Data
Model fitted on 428 real summer moves · tested once on 142 unseen moves
