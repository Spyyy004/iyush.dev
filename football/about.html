Why

One question, asked properly

Football talk is full of “big-game players”, home specialists and can't-miss signings. This project asks one question — what actually belongs to the player? — and follows it through five studies on every player-match in Europe's top five leagues from 2015/16 to 2024/25.

How it's built

Research first, interface second

The research runs as a separate Python pipeline. This site only reads its validated outputs: the Similarity Explorer and Transfer Calculator show results precomputed by the studies' own engines, and every frozen number is checked against the research at build time. It's a static site with no framework. How it's built on GitHub ↗

Kept current

Re-tested every Monday

Every Monday the pipeline adds the weekend's matches and re-tests the home-advantage and opposition findings on seasons the studies never saw. The frozen results never change.
