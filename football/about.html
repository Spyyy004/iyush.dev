About

I'm Ayush.

Product engineer by trade, football obsessive by choice. I build systems for answering football questions with data.

GitHub LinkedIn iyush.dev

Why

One question, asked properly

Football talk is full of “big-game players”, home specialists and can't-miss signings. This project asks one question — what actually belongs to the player? — and follows it through five studies on every player-match in Europe's top five leagues since 2015/16.

How it's built

Research first, interface second

The research runs as a separate pipeline. This site only reads its precomputed, validated outputs; the similarity search and transfer calculator are browser ports of the study models, tested against the originals. It's a static site with no framework and no build step.

Kept current

Rebuilt every Monday

Every Monday the pipeline adds the weekend's matches, re-tests the home-advantage and opposition findings on seasons the studies never saw, refreshes the similarity profiles and rebuilds the data behind this site. The frozen results never change.
